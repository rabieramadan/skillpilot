Our AI Applications

Innovative AI-powered solutions designed to transform various sectors and enhance operational efficiency

AlignOQF

AI-based OQF (Oman Qualification Framework) alignment checker that ensures educational programs meet national standards and quality requirements.

BalanceMate HR & Payroll

Comprehensive AI-powered HR and payroll management system that streamlines workforce management, attendance tracking, and salary processing.

SkillPilot

AI-driven training platform that delivers personalized learning experiences, adaptive content, and intelligent skill development pathways.

EthicSense Certificate

Innovative testing and certification platform for AI ethics, ensuring professionals understand ethical implications and responsible AI practices.

SkillMatch

Advanced skill gap analysis tool that identifies competency gaps and recommends targeted training programs for career advancement.

WorkShop

Specialized platform designed for managing workshops, training sessions, and issuing AI-related certificates with intelligent tracking and assessment.

WisdomPanel

Multi-agent advisory system featuring 30+ expert AI perspectives with dialogue capabilities for comprehensive consultation and decision-making support.

About Future Coverage

Powered by the AI Applications Chair at University of Nizwa

Leading AI Innovation in Oman

Future Coverage is the commercial arm of the AI Applications Chair (AIAC) at the University of Nizwa. We specialize in delivering world-class AI training programs and developing innovative AI applications that address real-world challenges.

Our mission is to empower individuals and organizations with cutting-edge AI knowledge and tools, fostering innovation and digital transformation across Oman and the GCC region.
